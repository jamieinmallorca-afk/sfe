# sfe — Privacy Notice

## Who we are

sfe is a pilot service that connects people who feel unsafe with vetted volunteer protectors, who stay with you by voice while you wait for help to arrive. It supports the emergency number 112 and does not replace it. Call 112 first.

The controller of your personal data is [name of the non-profit association], tax ID [CIF], [registered address], Palma, Illes Balears, Spain. For privacy questions write to [privacy email address].

This notice explains what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It applies to callers (people asking for support) and to protectors (volunteers).

We do not keep copies of ID documents, service records or criminal-record paperwork in the app. We record only that a check was completed.

We also record whether you told us you had called 112 during an alert, so that we can check the service supports the emergency number and does not replace it.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

## Sensitive information in alerts and calls

What you type in a note or say on a call may reveal sensitive things, such as your health, relationships or safety. By typing or saying it, you agree that we and the protector may handle it to help you in that call. Please share only what you need to.

Calls are not recorded and sfe does not store audio. Voice is encrypted and travels between the two devices, or through a relay server that cannot listen. Protectors are forbidden from recording, taking screenshots or keeping notes. We cannot stop this technically on their device, so anyone who does it is removed.

Your note is saved with the alert record. Before a protector answers, all available protectors can see your first name and your note.

## Who sees your data

- **Protectors.** Before one answers, every available protector sees your first name and your note. The protector who answers also sees your location, if you shared it. They never see your email, surname or phone number, and you never see theirs.
- **The sfe team.** Our administrators see accounts, vetting status, alert records and reports. Access is limited to people who need it to run the service and keep people safe [list roles].
- **Hosting and service providers.** Railway hosts the app and acts on our instructions under a data processing agreement [confirm].
- **Email and sign-in providers.** Resend sends our confirmation and password-reset emails, and Google provides "Continue with Google" if you use it. Both receive your email address and act under their own terms [confirm data processing agreements and transfer mechanism].
- **Authorities.** Only if the law requires it or someone's life is at risk.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

- show you the data we hold about you (access);
- correct anything wrong (rectification);
- delete your data (erasure);
- limit how we use it (restriction);
- give you a copy you can move elsewhere (portability);
- stop using it for our legitimate interests (objection).

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. For location, switch sharing off before pressing SOS or delete your account. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.

In the app you can download your data and delete your account yourself (Account section). For any other request write to [privacy email address]. We reply within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

## Security, known limits and age

sfe uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, stores passwords in scrambled form, and limits admin access. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell the Spanish data protection authority within 72 hours and tell you where the law requires it.

This is a pilot, so please know its limits:

- To set up a voice call, your device and the protector's device exchange network addresses. This can reveal your approximate location to the other person's device. We plan to route calls through a relay server to reduce this.
- We forbid protectors from recording calls, but cannot stop it technically.
- The app keeps you signed in by storing a login code in your browser. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.
- Alerts only reach protectors while their app is open and online.

sfe is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to use it.
