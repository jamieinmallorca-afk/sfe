# sfe — Protector Agreement

## Parties and purpose

This agreement is between [legal name of the organisation running sfe], tax ID [CIF], [address] ("sfe"), and you, the person who accepts it when joining sfe as a protector ("you").

You offer some of your free time to talk by voice with people who feel unsafe or need someone to talk to, through the sfe app and under the rules below.

## Your role

You take voice calls through the app from people who feel unsafe. You listen, help the person feel calmer, ask what is happening and where they are, and help them decide what to do next.

**If the person is in danger or needs medical help, tell them to call 112 straight away. Never delay this.**

You never:

- go to the caller's location or arrange to meet;
- ask for or give phone numbers, social media or any personal contact details;
- give medical, legal or psychological advice beyond listening and calming;
- promise outcomes, or contact the caller outside the app;
- accept money, gifts or favours;
- record, screenshot or write down details of a call.

You may end a call at any time if you feel unsafe or uncomfortable.

You choose when to switch on "Available". [Time commitment: none; you decide.] Once you answer, please stay on the call until it reaches a natural end or the person has reached help, unless you must stop.

## Vetting and training

You can take calls only when sfe has completed four checks and approved you:

1. **Identity** verified.
2. **Criminal-record declaration:** you declare in writing that you have no uncancelled convictions for the offences listed in Art. 8.5 of Ley 45/2015. These include domestic or gender violence, crimes against the life, physical integrity, liberty, moral integrity or sexual freedom of a spouse or children, human trafficking, and terrorism [exact wording to be confirmed].
3. **Two references** checked.
4. **Training completed:** [2 hours] on de-escalation, listening, when to say "call 112 now", boundaries and data protection, with a short test and supervised first calls.

You must be at least 21. Tell us promptly if anything you declared changes. We repeat the checks every 12 months. We keep only a record that each check was completed, not copies of your documents.

## Conduct, safeguarding and escalation

Be respectful and calm. No discrimination, no sexual or romantic remarks, no pressure, and no promoting your own beliefs, products or services.

If you hear that someone is being harmed or is at serious risk, including a child or someone who may hurt themselves, tell them to call 112. If they are in immediate danger and cannot call, you may call 112 yourself and give the location shown in the app [safety manager to confirm this policy].

Report any problem, such as an abusive caller or something that worries you after a call, through the app or to the safety manager at [phone/contact, available around the clock]. After a hard call you can ask for a debrief. You may stop volunteering at any time.

Breaking these rules can lead to immediate removal and, where appropriate, reporting to the authorities.

## Confidentiality and data protection

Keep everything you learn on a call confidential, during and after your time as a protector. Do not identify callers or discuss calls with anyone, except the safety manager when needed.

Do not record, screenshot, photograph, transcribe or keep notes of a call. If something is captured by accident, delete it and tell us.

Use caller information only to help during the call. Never look callers up or contact them, including on social media.

Use only your own account, never share your login, lock your device and log out on shared devices. If a device is lost or you think data has leaked, tell us within [24 hours], because we must report some breaches to the authorities within 72 hours.

sfe decides how caller data is used and you handle it on sfe's instructions. Confidentiality never stops you calling 112 or telling the safety manager about danger. See the sfe Privacy Notice for how your own data is used.

## No pay, no employment, insurance and liability

Protecting is unpaid. [Expenses: sfe does not reimburse expenses / sfe reimburses the following expenses: list.]

You are not an employee of sfe. You choose when to be available, and sfe cannot require you to take calls or work set hours.

sfe will take out accident and illness insurance for protectors, and civil liability cover where required, at its own expense [confirm policy and provider]. You will receive the policy details before your first call, and you will not be asked to take calls until it is in place.

While you act as a protector within this agreement, sfe is responsible towards third parties for damage you cause by mistake [confirm]. This does not cover deliberate misconduct or acting outside this agreement.

You have the rights Ley 45/2015 gives to volunteers, including to be informed, trained, respected and not discriminated against, and to receive a certificate of your service on request.

## Ending, suspension, law and acceptance

**Duration.** [Open-ended / 12 months, renewable.] You can leave at any time by switching off Available and telling us in writing. sfe can suspend or end this agreement at once if rules are broken, someone may be at risk, a yearly re-check is failed, or sfe stops running the service.

**Changes.** Any change to your role or to this agreement is made in writing and agreed by both sides.

**Disputes.** First talk to [contact]. If that fails, [mediation / the courts of Palma de Mallorca]. Spanish law applies [confirm].

**Acceptance.** By ticking the box in the app you accept this agreement. The date, time and version of your acceptance are recorded. [A signed copy will be exchanged before your first live call.]
